### 5C. DYNAMIC HINTS (Qwen Fallback)
When no pattern matches and generic hints aren't sufficient, the system sends the problem details to Qwen for a custom hint. This adds 2-4 seconds latency but provides truly intelligent, problem-specific guidance.

**Qwen receives:**
- The math problem
- The student's incorrect answer
- The correct answer
- The grade level
- The TEKS standard being tested

**Qwen generates:** A targeted, Jarvis-voiced hint specific to the mistake. Then Cartesia voices it.
